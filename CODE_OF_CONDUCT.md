# Code of conduct

CourseCraft is built by people who make learning for others. Treat everyone here the way you'd want a learner treated.

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in their second language.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Explain why, with examples.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or unwanted personal attention, in any space connected to the project.
- Don't share other people's private information or client material.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who keep breaking them. To report a problem with someone's behaviour, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub.
